About BuildEstimate

BuildEstimate is a focused collection of free home-project calculators. Our goal is to make common material calculations easier to understand and check.

What we publish

We currently cover concrete, drywall, fencing and gravel. Each calculator shows or explains the underlying geometry and keeps assumptions visible where possible.

How calculations are prepared

We use standard arithmetic and geometry, plus clearly identified planning assumptions. Where manufacturer-specific yield or material density matters, we tell users to verify the exact product or supplier data.

What we are not

BuildEstimate is not an engineering, architecture, building-code or contractor service. Our results are planning estimates and are not a substitute for project specifications, local codes or qualified professional advice.

Editorial approach

Guides are written to support the tools directly. We do not publish unrelated topics simply to increase page count. We update calculators when formulas, assumptions or supporting references materially change.
