Privacy Policy

Effective: September 20, 2026.

Calculator inputs

The launch version performs calculations in your browser. It does not require an account and does not intentionally send calculator measurements to a BuildEstimate server.

Hosting and logs

The site may be hosted through a third-party provider such as GitHub Pages. That provider may process standard technical request data according to its own policies.

Analytics and advertising

The launch build does not include Google Analytics or AdSense code. If analytics or advertising is added later, this policy must be updated before deployment and any required consent mechanisms must be implemented.

External links

Links to external websites are governed by those sites' privacy practices.
