Cubic Yards Explained for Home Projects

Learn what a cubic yard is, how to convert from cubic feet and why concrete, gravel, mulch and soil are often measured by volume.

Published September 20, 2026 · BuildEstimate Editorial Team
How this guide was prepared: We use standard geometry and clearly stated planning assumptions. Where product yield matters, verify the exact manufacturer label before purchase. BuildEstimate is not an engineering or building-code authority.

What is a cubic yard?

A cubic yard is the volume of a cube that is one yard long, one yard wide and one yard high. Because one yard equals three feet, one cubic yard contains 27 cubic feet.

1 yd³ = 27 ft³

Convert cubic feet to cubic yards

Divide cubic feet by 27. For example, 54 ft³ equals 2 yd³.

Why home projects use cubic yards

Bulk materials such as ready-mix concrete, gravel, mulch and soil are often easier to quote by cubic yard than by hundreds of cubic feet. The unit is large enough to match common delivery quantities.

Thickness must use the same unit

A frequent error is multiplying feet by feet by inches. Convert inches to feet first by dividing by 12, then calculate cubic feet.

Volume is not weight

A cubic yard of one material does not weigh the same as a cubic yard of another. If you need tons, use an appropriate density for the material.
