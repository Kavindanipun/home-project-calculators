How this drywall calculator works

The tool starts with the area of the four walls: two times the room length plus two times the room width, multiplied by wall height. If the ceiling option is selected, it adds length × width. Door and window areas are then subtracted.

Drywall area formula

Wall area = 2 × (length + width) × height
Total board area = wall area + ceiling area − openings

The waste allowance is applied before the area is divided by the selected sheet size. The final sheet count is rounded up.

Example

A 12 × 10 ft room with 8 ft walls has 352 ft² of wall surface before openings. Add the ceiling and subtract doors and windows to get the net board area.

Common questions

Should I subtract every opening?

For larger doors and windows, subtraction improves the estimate. Small openings may not materially change the number of full sheets because offcuts and layout still matter.

Why is waste included?

Sheets are cut to fit edges, corners and openings. A waste allowance helps account for pieces that cannot be reused efficiently.
