How this fence calculator works

The post estimate is based on total fence length and the post spacing you enter. Gate width is removed from the picket and rail run, while additional gate-post allowance is added to the post estimate.

Fence material formulas

Approx. sections = net fence length ÷ post spacing
Pickets = net fence length ÷ (picket width + gap), then add waste

This is a planning model, not a substitute for a site layout. Corners, slopes, transitions and gate construction can all change material counts.

Example: 120 ft privacy fence

With 8 ft post spacing, one 4 ft gate and 5.5 in pickets, the calculator estimates the fence run after the gate, then determines sections, rails and pickets.

Common questions

Is 8 ft post spacing always correct?

No. Use the spacing specified for your fence system, site conditions and local requirements. The calculator makes spacing editable for that reason.

Does the post count include gates?

It includes a simple gate-post allowance. Complex or double gates may need a different layout.
