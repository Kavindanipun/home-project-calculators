How to estimate concrete bags

First calculate the required concrete volume. Then divide that volume by the approximate yield of the selected bag size and round up. A fraction of a bag is not useful when you are buying whole bags.

Bag formula

Bags needed = concrete volume including waste ÷ yield per bag

This calculator includes published approximate yields for 40, 60 and 80 lb QUIKRETE Concrete Mix bags. Product formulations differ, so the bag label is the authoritative source for the product you actually purchase.

Why bag size matters

The same project may require many more 40 lb bags than 80 lb bags. Smaller bags can be easier to handle, while larger bags reduce the number of individual bags. Cost per cubic foot can also vary by brand and store.

Common questions

Why does the calculator round up?

Because concrete is sold in whole bags. Rounding down could leave the project short.

Can I compare cost?

Yes. Enter the current price per bag to get a simple material-cost estimate. It does not include tax, delivery, tools or reinforcement.
